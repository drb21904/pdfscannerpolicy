Privacy Policy for PrivacyFirst PDF Scanner

Last updated: October 8, 2026

PrivacyFirst PDF Scanner ("the app") is a document scanner for Android, developed by Privacy First Technologies. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Summary

Your documents never leave your phone unless you choose to send them. The app has no user accounts, no analytics and no server of its own. Scanning, text reading, Safe share and the PDF tools all work on your device. To keep the app free, it shows a few small, non-personalised banner ads from Google AdMob; the only data that leaves your device for this is what Google's ad service receives in order to show those ads (see "Advertising" below).

What stays on your phone

  • The pictures you scan, the pages made from them, and the text read from them are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The app doesn't upload them or back them up to any cloud, and other apps can't see them. The app's data is excluded from Android's cloud backup.
  • Text recognition runs entirely on your device, using Google ML Kit through Google Play services and the open-source Tesseract engine. Your pictures aren't sent to any server. Finding a page's edges and making ID-photo backgrounds use Google's on-device ML Kit in the same way.
  • Safe share (hiding ID, card and account numbers before you share) works on your device. The numbers it finds are never stored separately or sent anywhere.
  • Settings you choose (such as language, look and app lock) are stored on your device only.

Finding documents already on your phone

  • "Find documents in gallery" can look through folders you pick for photos of documents, PDFs and Word or text files. You pick the folders, or single files, in Android's own picker. The app has no permission to see anything else on your phone and does not ask for storage or "All files access" permission. Removing a folder from the list gives up access to it.
  • Files are checked on your phone only when you ask the app to search, and are never uploaded. The app doesn't change or delete them: the documents it makes are copies kept inside the app.
  • When you import pictures or PDFs, the app receives only the items you select in Android's photo or file picker.

Files the app downloads

Some features download free files the first time you use them: extra text-recognition languages (from the open-source Tesseract project on GitHub), and the search-by-meaning model and translation language packs (from Google). Only the file is downloaded. Nothing about you or your documents is sent.

When documents leave your phone

Only when you choose to share, save or print a document, and only to the app, folder or printer you pick, using Android's standard share sheet, file picker or print service. The app does not see or control what another app does with a file afterwards; that is governed by the other app's own privacy policy.

What the app doesn't collect

The app has no user accounts. It doesn't collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location, and it does not use analytics services. The only third-party SDK that sends data is the Google Mobile Ads SDK, described below.

Advertising

The app shows small banner ads provided by Google AdMob on a few screens. They never appear on the camera or editor screens, and there are no full-screen or video ads.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and sends to Google: your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate its general location), device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, interactions with the ads, and diagnostic information about ad performance. This data is encrypted in transit and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The app only requests non-personalised ads: ads are not selected on the basis of your interests, your activity or a profile built about you. Google may still use the data above for limited purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated ad reporting and fraud prevention. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. No document, picture, file name or text is ever given to the ad service.

Removing ads (optional purchase)

You can remove the ads with a one-time purchase through Google Play. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the app never sees your payment details and only learns whether the purchase was made.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a short technical report is kept on your phone. It is never sent automatically; you decide whether to copy and share it.

Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions, each used only for its stated purpose:

  • Camera — to scan documents, QR codes and ID photos. Pictures stay on your device.
  • Biometric / fingerprint — only if you turn on App lock, to unlock the app with your fingerprint or face. The app never receives your biometric data; Android only tells it whether unlocking succeeded.
  • Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 9 and older only) — to save a PDF to Downloads or keep PDF copies in Documents when you ask. Newer Android versions need no storage permission for this.
  • Internet and network state — to load ads and to download the optional language and search files described above. Your documents are never sent over the network by the app.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID and related ad-services permissions) — added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, used only to show and measure ads as described above.
  • Google Play billing — for the optional "Remove ads" purchase.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Deleted documents go to the Recycle bin and are permanently removed after 30 days, or sooner if you empty the bin.
  • By default, PDF copies of your documents are kept in Documents/PrivacyFirst PDF Scanner on your phone (you can turn this off in Settings). They stay there if you uninstall the app, can be restored into the app after reinstalling, and are visible to file manager apps on your phone.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently deletes the documents stored inside the app. Because the app has no accounts or servers, there is no copy of your data held anywhere else by us.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children. The app itself collects no personal information; ads are served by Google AdMob as described above.

Not affiliated with any government body
